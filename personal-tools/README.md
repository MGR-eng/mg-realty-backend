# Personal Tools

Standalone tools saved here are not part of the CRM backend — open them directly in a browser.

## Budget Tracker
**File:** `budget-tracker.html`
**Open:** Double-click the file, or drag it into Chrome/Safari
**Data:** Pulls from the CRM backend (`mg-realty-backend.onrender.com`) — expenses are shared with Ace's receipt tracking, so anything you text Ace shows up here automatically
**Categories:** Rent ($2,600) · Phone ($245) · Groceries ($350) · Dining ($300) · Transportation ($150) · Fitness ($70) · Subscriptions ($175) · Business ($150) · Personal ($100) · Other ($60)
**Monthly income:** $4,400

To update budgets or income, edit the `INCOME` and `BUDGETS` constants at the top of the `<script>` block.
