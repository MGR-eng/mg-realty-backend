## WEEK 1 — The Introduction
**Theme: "Why WeHo, Why Now"**

### Feed Post (Carousel — 5 slides)
**Slide 1 — Cover:**
Big, clean photo of the Sunset Strip at golden hour or the Pacific Design Center.
Text overlay: *"West Hollywood. July's Neighborhood of Note."*

**Slide 2 — The Take:**
> "Most people think of WeHo as a nightlife neighborhood. What they miss is that it's quietly one of the most walkable, livable, and underrated value plays in all of Los Angeles right now. Let me show you why."

**Slide 3 — Three things buyers don't know:**
> 1. WeHo is its own city — not part of LA. That means its own city services, faster permitting, and a local government that's actually responsive.
> 2. You can walk to dinner. In LA. Let that sink in.
> 3. The design district on Melrose and the PDC bring a creative, high-income crowd that keeps property values stable even when the broader market shifts.

**Slide 4 — The vibe:**
> "Think: morning coffee at Alfred Coffee on Melrose, brunch at Bottega Louie, dinner within walking distance. This is the neighborhood for people who are done commuting to their own life."

**Slide 5 — CTA:**
> "I'm spending the month in WeHo. Follow along — and if you've been thinking about this neighborhood, DM me. I know every block."
