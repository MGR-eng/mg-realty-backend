About Matt

Passion, honesty, and trust.

My career started in television development — working with studios, networks, and agencies to find projects worth making and figure out how to get them made. That meant reading between the lines, negotiating hard, knowing what something was actually worth versus what someone was asking for it, and understanding people well enough to know what they really wanted before they said it out loud.

Those skills don't stay in the industry. They follow you everywhere — especially into real estate.

I moved to Los Angeles from Atlanta in 2009 and spent the next decade and a half learning this city the way you only can when you chose it. Block by block, neighborhood by neighborhood. I know why Silver Lake trades the way it does, why one street in Los Feliz outperforms the next, why Hollywood Hills pricing looks chaotic until you understand the canyon dynamics.

Today I work with buyers and sellers across LA who want a clear read on the market and someone who'll advocate for them without the performance. The development background taught me that — the best deals happen when everyone's working from honest information.
